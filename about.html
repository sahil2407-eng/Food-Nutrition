Our Mission

What We Stand For

"Our mission is to make reliable, science-backed food and nutrition information freely accessible to every Indian consumer, helping people make safer, healthier, and more informed choices."

NutriGuard started as a response to a simple observation: most people want to eat well, but understanding food labels, nutrition facts, and safety standards is genuinely hard. We bridge that gap with plain-language explanations backed by established scientific sources.

Background

Why We Built NutriGuard

India's food landscape is vast and diverse — but it comes with challenges that affect every household.

🏷️

Label Complexity

Food labels are packed with information — serving sizes, % daily values, E-numbers — that most consumers find confusing or misleading. NutriGuard breaks these down in plain language.

⚠️

Adulteration Concerns

Food adulteration remains a significant public health challenge. While we cannot test products ourselves, we educate consumers on warning signs, common adulterants, and how to report concerns to FSSAI.

📊

Nutrition Awareness Gap

Chronic diseases linked to poor nutrition — obesity, diabetes, hypertension — are rising across India. Better nutrition awareness at the consumer level is one of the most impactful preventive tools available.

🔍

Information Accessibility

Scientific nutrition data exists — from ICMR-NIN, USDA, WHO — but it's scattered across research papers and government portals. NutriGuard centralises and simplifies this for everyday use.

Data Sources

Where Our Data Comes From

NutriGuard curates information from respected, authoritative sources. We reference these organisations for nutritional values, safety standards, and dietary guidance.

Primary

ICMR-NIN

Indian Council of Medical Research – National Institute of Nutrition. Our primary reference for Indian food composition data, RDA (Recommended Dietary Allowances), and nutrient profiles for traditional Indian foods.

Visit ICMR-NIN
Global

USDA FoodData Central

US Department of Agriculture's comprehensive food composition database. Used as a cross-reference for packaged and processed foods, especially international brands available in India.

Visit USDA FDC
Regulatory

FSSAI

Food Safety and Standards Authority of India. Our reference for Indian food safety regulations, permissible additive limits, labelling standards, and permitted food colours and preservatives.

Visit FSSAI
Global Health

WHO Nutrition Guidance

World Health Organization nutrition guidelines. Used for sugar intake recommendations, salt targets, fat consumption guidance, and global dietary best practices.

Visit WHO
Important Limitations

What NutriGuard Is Not

Transparency matters. Please read these important caveats before relying on NutriGuard data.

📋

Demo data only: NutriGuard shows curated demo data for educational purposes. Always verify nutritional values and safety information with actual product labels and official sources.

🏛️

No government affiliation: We are not affiliated with FSSAI, ICMR, the Ministry of Health, or any government body. We are an independent educational project.

🔬

No testing or verification: We do not detect adulteration, verify product authenticity, or endorse any brand. Laboratory testing is the only reliable method for adulteration detection.

🩺

Not medical advice: Nothing on NutriGuard constitutes medical or dietary advice. Consult a registered dietitian or physician for personal health and nutrition guidance.
