💡 Quick Tips

Smarter Shopping Starts Here

🧾

Always Read Labels

Check serving size first — nutrition numbers may look small but often refer to a fraction of the pack.

🔢

Compare Per 100g

Always compare products on a per-100g basis for a fair apples-to-apples nutritional comparison.

🧂

Watch for Hidden Salt

Sodium can hide in bread, cereals, sauces, and biscuits. Aim for under 2,000 mg per day.

🌾

Whole Grains First

Choose whole wheat, brown rice, or oats over refined options for more fibre and micronutrients.
